# HSH — Brand guide

## The mark: “The Canvas”

HSH is cut out of a solid plate. The plate is the ring canvas seen from above; the letters exist only as absence — a seal, not a monogram.

**The hidden story**

- **The plate** is the ring — the square of canvas where every chapter (chess board, pitch, pool, mat, ring, screen) happened.
- **The S is a rope under tension.** Its two tails *are* the crossbars of both H's, so the S literally holds Hojiakbar and Sharofiddinov together.
- **180° rotational symmetry.** Turn the mark upside down and it still reads HSH — balance, the stance of two fighters facing each other, discipline that looks the same from either corner.
- **A 12-unit grid**, the same rigour as a chessboard: nothing is placed by eye.
- **Ring → code.** In motion the mark becomes ring ropes in the boxing chapter and a `< >` bracket in the work section.

## Construction

```
viewBox 0 0 132 72  ·  grid unit 12  ·  cut width 8  ·  corner radius 10

plate   132 × 72 (11 × 6 units)
stems   x = 14, 38 | 94, 118     y = 12 → 60   (4 units of air top and bottom)
spine   M14 48 H54  A12 12 0 1 0 66 36  A12 12 0 1 1 78 24 H118
        left crossbar (y 48) → two r = 12 bowls centred on (66, 36) → right crossbar (y 24)
```

Compact mark (favicon, collapsed header): 72 × 72, radius 12, cut 9 — inner stems + spine only.

Source of truth: `src/components/logo/logoPaths.ts`.

## Clear space and size

- Clear space on every side: **half the plate height** (36 units).
- Full mark minimum: **32 px wide** on screen, **12 mm** in print.
- Below 32 px use the **compact mark**; it holds down to 16 px.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0B0B0C` | plate on light |
| Paper | `#F4F2EE` | plate on dark |
| Ash | `#8A8780` / `#96938C` | secondary type |
| Champagne | `#C9B48A` | light sweep, impact ring, animated favicon only |

The plate is always one solid colour; the letters are always the background showing through.

## Lockups

- **Horizontal:** mark + “Hojiakbar Sharofiddinov” in Clash Display 600, gap = ½ plate height.
- **Stacked:** mark above the name set on two lines, left-aligned.

## Do / Don't

**Do** keep the plate solid and the cuts empty · use the compact mark below 32 px · place it on flat Ink or Paper · animate it with the states below.

**Don't** outline the plate · fill the cuts with another colour · add gradients or shadows · rotate it by anything other than 180° · stretch it · set the champagne accent as the plate colour (except the animated favicon).

## Motion states (`<HSHLogo />`)

| State | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Intro** | The sport story draws inside the plate bounds — chessboard → pitch lines → water ripple → ring ropes → code cursor — then the plate snaps in, the cuts draw, and it lands with a punch (squash + release) and a champagne shockwave. |
| **FLIP** | After the intro the preloader's mark flies into the header slot as the curtain splits. |
| **Idle** | A slow champagne light sweep crosses the plate every ~7 s. Paused off-screen and in hidden tabs. |
| **Hover** | The S swings half a turn (it lands identical — the symmetry pays off) while the two H's part and re-join; magnetic pull with elastic return. |
| **Click** | Punch: compress, release, shockwave; scrolls to top. |
| **Scroll** | Skews with scroll velocity; collapses to the compact mark on scroll down, returns on scroll up. |
| **Section-aware** | Boxing chapter → the S becomes ring ropes; Selected works → a `< >` bracket. |
| **Transitions** | Flashes at the centre of the page-transition wipe. |
| **Easter egg** | Type `hsh` anywhere: full-screen performance of the intro. |
| **Favicon** | Canvas-driven: the champagne plate's S swings every 4 s. Static `favicon.svg` fallback; the tab title becomes “Come back 👊” when hidden. |
| **Reduced motion** | Static final mark, no idle loop, no story. |
